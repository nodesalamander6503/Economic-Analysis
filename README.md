# Analysis1

A simple analysis of a few markets.
I elected to focus on subsets of the S&P 500, to serve as a conventional market to analyze, as well as Hypixel Skyblock's Bazaar, as a more unusual market which may have slight differences in its properties.
I simply tested intra-item correlations and inter-item correlations, as a way to show that a certain set of items has neither inter-relation nor temporal relation.
